## Companion design

Part A records initial installation and service verification against prescribed instructions. Part B organizes only the checks required before an outing. It must not imply unpacking/reinstalling on every outing unless the manual requires it.

Six areas: equipment/instruction match; installation verification; condition/service; fit/wearing procedure; registration/service/arming; unresolved issues and the relevant official instruction. Every area begins **Not verified**. A person may record **Checked** or **Needs attention** with an instruction reference and observation. “Checked” is a user report, not an automatic diagnostic. There is no overall green, safe, or ready result.

The companion adds no physical components to deployment mechanisms and has no emergency phone/app dependency. It cannot prove inflation, flotation, communications delivery, or rescue.

## Comfort exploration

Use unmodified reference equipment for dry seated boating and paddling motions. Record neck pressure, arm restriction, seat interference, heat, and adjustment access. Identify specific problems before proposing changes to textiles, materials, or flotation geometry. See the [observation form](observation-form.md).
