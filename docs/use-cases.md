# Vest use-case catalog

[Home](../README.md) · [Visual overview](overview.md) · [Decisions](decisions.md) · [Next steps](next-steps.md)

Updated November 9, 2026. Keep broad ideas visible while distinguishing their maturity and evidence needs. These are research scenarios, not capabilities of one existing device. No Vest safety benefit has been demonstrated.

| Use case | Desired outcome | Current maturity / document |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Child approaching water — highest-priority track, first research phase** | Warn a supervising caregiver before a young child who cannot yet swim reaches a defined water hazard. | Draft [warning requirements and review package](child-warning-requirements.md); no working device or demonstrated benefit: [child water safety](child-water-proximity-use-case.md). |
| **Child accidentally enters water — part of the same child-safety goal** | Provide child-appropriate flotation to help keep the child afloat and urgent caregiver alerts to support a prompt response. | Explicit intended function; separate pediatric requirements and validation still needed. Not covered by the warning-only review package: [after-entry protection](child-water-proximity-use-case.md). |
| Child left in a hot car | Detect possible unattended occupancy and heat risk, alert caregivers, and escalate to emergency services through a supported path. | Initial research brief; no pediatric device, thresholds, or emergency integration validated: [child hot-car alerts](child-hot-car-use-case.md). |
| Dog left in a hot car | Recognize possible unattended occupancy and heat risk, alert a responsible person, and reach appropriate emergency responders. | Separate canine research and response plan; pediatric thresholds do not transfer: [dog hot-car alerts](dog-hot-car-use-case.md). |
| Harmful-water exposure | Explore sensing specified bacteria, toxins, or contaminants and alerting a wearer or caregiver. | Initial feasibility brief; immediate universal detection is not established: [water-quality alerts](water-quality-use-case.md). |
| Unexpected lake immersion | Support adult wear, fit, readiness, and hands-free alerting when the wearer cannot act. | Earlier adult dry usability project, now secondary: [water prototype](prototype.md). |
| Avalanche involvement and burial | Reduce burial, create breathing space, and explore automatic activation or breathable-air support. | Research brief with existing-equipment comparisons: [avalanche](avalanche-use-case.md). |
| Fall from a horse | Cushion relevant impacts during a rider fall. | Research comparison; no equipment selected: [fall protection](fall-impact-use-case.md). |
| Motorcycle crash or fall | Recognize a relevant event and cushion impact in time. | Separate activity-specific research scenario: [fall protection](fall-impact-use-case.md). |
| Fall from altitude | Investigate wearable deceleration and/or cushioning that might improve survivability. | Explicit long-term stretch goal, retained at the user's request: [altitude research](fall-impact-use-case.md). |
| Other rapid falls | Explore additional contexts where automatic cushioning could help. | Broad idea awaiting a defined activity, user, and impact scenario: [fall protection](fall-impact-use-case.md). |
| Horse-to-rider early warning | A horse-worn monitor communicates with the vest to warn of a possible impending spook. | Prediction, communication, and safe response remain separate hypotheses: [horse warning](horse-warning-use-case.md). |
| Horse stress monitoring and calming | A horse-worn vest and monitor detect stress and investigate suitable calming modalities, aiming to reduce spooking or bucking. | Separate intervention research; no automated calming or rider-protection benefit established: [horse calming](horse-calming-use-case.md). |
| Emergency dismount assistance | Explore whether vest cushioning could reduce injury during an emergency dismount. | Stretch concept linked to the horse warning; no claim that jumping is safe: [horse warning](horse-warning-use-case.md). |
| Obstacle sensing and navigation assistance | Help blind and low-vision wearers perceive surrounding obstacles through sensors and accessible vest feedback. | Open concept with hummingbird sensory and robotics references; nighttime forest running remains a long-term ambition: [obstacle navigation](obstacle-navigation-use-case.md). |

Child water safety before and after entry takes precedence over the other tracks. Approach-warning requirements remain the first research phase; they do not substitute for the separate work needed on flotation and after-entry alerts. The next steps are requirements review and feasibility evaluation, not child deployment. The earlier adult study and other scenarios remain in the catalog without treating them as ready for fabrication or human testing. New cases should record the user need, existing approaches, evidence gaps, and the next safe way to learn.
