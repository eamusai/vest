# Vest

Help save even one additional life by making useful water-safety designs easier to understand, evaluate, and eventually reproduce.

**Status — November 8, 2026:** research and human-factors drafts only. No equipment has been purchased or modified, no prototype fabricated, no participants recruited, and no experiments conducted. No safety benefit has been demonstrated. This repository is private; nothing here has been published.

The working scenario is an adult recreational boater or canoeist on a lake who unexpectedly enters the water and cannot use a phone or press a distress button. The first project is a printable preparation companion and dry comfort/usability research using existing equipment. It adds no hardware to a deployment mechanism and needs no phone in an emergency.

The companion is an incomplete research mock-up, not an operational checklist or certification of readiness. Exact manufacturer requirements must be mapped and reviewed before participant testing. Follow the current instructions for the actual equipment; these drafts do not replace them.

## Sharing and licensing

The intended direction is to share tested, reproducible work with capable manufacturers and researchers, rather than pursue patents. No license has been selected and this initially empty repository contained none. Do not describe it as legally open-source yet. Manufacturer manuals and drawings are linked, not copied into this repository. Public release requires a separate decision.
